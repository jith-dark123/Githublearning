# Billing FAQ

## Invoice timing
Enterprise subscription invoices are issued on the first business day of each month and are due within 30 calendar days. Customers can download PDF and CSV invoice details from the billing workspace.

## Disputes
Billing disputes should be opened within 60 days of the invoice date. The billing operations team acknowledges a new dispute within two business days and targets resolution within ten business days.

## Credits
Approved service credits are applied to the next invoice rather than paid out as cash unless the contract explicitly states otherwise.
