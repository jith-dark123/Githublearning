# Product Guide

## Analytics workspace
The analytics workspace provides saved queries, dashboard views, scheduled exports, and role-based sharing. Users can export a dashboard result as CSV or PNG. Scheduled reports support daily, weekly, and monthly delivery.

## Search
The global search bar searches dashboard titles, metric definitions, and help-center documents. Search does not inspect private notes owned by another user.

## Availability
The platform targets 99.9 percent monthly availability for enterprise plans, excluding announced maintenance windows.
